Guides

What our scientists get asked most.

Critical quality attributes of mRNA therapeutics

Start from the product: the RNA, its impurities and the LNP. Each attribute links to safety or efficacy, and each needs a method with known capability.

Read the full guide ›

Why dsRNA matters and how it is measured

The J2 antibody recognises double-stranded regions of 40 base pairs or more. ELISA gives a number; dot blot gives a quick check. Purification by cellulose or HPLC reduces it.

Scaling analytics from clinical to commercial

Clinical methods are often research methods. Before commercial filing they need validation, transfer and robust reference materials. Plan this 12 to 18 months ahead.

Cold-chain handling for mRNA samples

Freeze once, aliquot once, log everything. Repeated freeze-thaw changes LNP size and EE, which can look like a stability failure.

FAQ

Questions, answered first.

What are the critical quality attributes of an mRNA product?#

Identity, integrity, capping, poly(A) tail, dsRNA and DNA template impurities, LNP size, PDI and encapsulation, lipid content, potency, and safety tests such as endotoxin and sterility.

Why does dsRNA matter?#

Double-stranded RNA formed during in vitro transcription activates innate immune sensors, which can reduce protein expression and cause reactogenicity.

How is capping efficiency measured?#

A probe directs RNase H to cut near the 5′ end; the short fragment is analysed by LC-MS to quantify Cap 1, Cap 0 and uncapped species.

What encapsulation efficiency is typical?#

Published processes commonly report above 90%. Your acceptance criterion comes from your clinical lots.

Can you test self-amplifying RNA?#

Yes. Longer constructs need adapted integrity and sequencing methods; tell us the length at quote stage.

How do you prevent RNase contamination?#

Dedicated benches, RNase-free consumables, routine surface checks and separate pipettes for RNA work.

Do you test conventional vaccines too?#

Yes: antigen content, adjuvant content, potency assays and safety tests, depending on the product.

How should mRNA samples be shipped?#

Usually on dry ice at −70 °C or colder, with a logger. We book the courier and track the shipment.
