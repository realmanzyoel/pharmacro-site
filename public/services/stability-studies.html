The short answer

Stability studies show how a product's quality changes over time under temperature, humidity and light. We store and test to ICH Q1A(R2) to Q1E and Q5C: long-term, intermediate and accelerated conditions in every climatic zone including Zone IVb (30 °C/75% RH), photostability, in-use, freeze-thaw and shipping studies, with trending and shelf-life estimation.

Guides

What our scientists get asked most.

Zone IVb for India and ASEAN launches

India and ASEAN countries require long-term data at 30 °C/75% RH. Products developed only at 25/60 often fail here on moisture-driven degradation or dissolution slowdown. Test the commercial pack early, and consider desiccants or Alu-Alu blisters before the registration batches.

Read the full guide ›

Stability protocol template library

Our templates cover new drug products, generics, APIs, biologics and post-approval changes. Each lists conditions, pull points, tests, acceptance criteria and the statistics plan, ready for your QA to adapt.

Handling an out-of-trend result

OOT is not OOS. Confirm the data first, check the method and the time point against regression prediction intervals, then decide whether a deeper investigation is needed. Document the rule you use before the study starts.

Live chamber dashboard

Every chamber reports temperature and humidity every minute. The portal shows your samples' chambers, the last 30 days of data and any excursion with its impact assessment.

FAQ

Questions, answered first.

Which conditions do I need for India and ASEAN?#

Long-term at 30 °C/75% RH (Zone IVb) plus accelerated at 40 °C/75% RH. Products also sold in Zone II markets often add 25 °C/60% RH.

How many batches does ICH require?#

At least three primary batches for registration, two of which at least pilot scale, manufactured by a process that simulates the final one.

What counts as significant change at accelerated?#

For a drug product: a 5% change in assay from initial, any degradant over its acceptance criterion, failure of appearance or physical attributes, pH, or dissolution for 12 units.

How long before I can file?#

ICH expects 12 months of long-term and 6 months of accelerated data at submission for most new products; some regions accept less for generics.

Can you extrapolate shelf-life?#

Within ICH Q1E limits: typically up to twice the long-term data, and not more than 12 months beyond it, when data show little change and statistics support it.

How are chamber excursions handled?#

Chambers are monitored every minute. An excursion triggers an alert, an impact assessment and a notification in your portal, with the temperature trace attached.

Do you run stability for biologics?#

Yes, under ICH Q5C, usually at 5 °C long-term with 25 °C accelerated and −20 °C or −70 °C for drug substance.

What is bracketing?#

Testing only the extremes of a design factor, such as the smallest and largest strength, on the assumption the middle behaves in between. ICH Q1D sets when it is acceptable.

Can I see my chamber data?#

Yes. The portal shows live and historical conditions for the chambers holding your samples.
