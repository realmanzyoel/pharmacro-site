Bioburden and microbial limits

Microbiology method to USP <61>, USP <62>, Ph. Eur. 2.6.12. Typical sample: 10 to 30 g or mL. Indicative turnaround: 5 to 8 business days.

Request this test Talk to a scientist

The short answer

Bioburden testing counts the micro-organisms in non-sterile products and pre-filtration samples and checks for specified organisms such as E. coli.

How it works

Total aerobic microbial count and total yeast and mould count are measured by plate count, membrane filtration or MPN. Tests for specified micro-organisms use selective enrichment. Method suitability confirms the product does not suppress recovery.

When you need it

  • Oral and topical product release
  • Pre-filtration bioburden for sterile manufacture
  • Raw materials

What you receive

  • TAMC, TYMC (CFU/g or mL)
  • Absence or presence of specified organisms
  • Suitability report

Limitations and common pitfalls

What goes wrong most often, and what we check for:

  • Antimicrobial products suppressing recovery
  • Sample hold times too long
  • Choosing wrong acceptance criteria for the dosage form

Questions

How much sample do you need for bioburden and microbial limits?#

10 to 30 g or mL. Exact amounts depend on your specification and the number of replicates; we confirm them in the quote.

How long does bioburden and microbial limits take?#

Typically 5 to 8 business days, from sample receipt and acceptance. Priority and Critical tiers are available for most methods. Indicative

Which standards does this method follow?#

USP <61>, USP <62>, Ph. Eur. 2.6.12, Ph. Eur. 2.6.13, JP 4.05. We run the compendial procedure where one exists, or a validated in-house method justified against it.

Is the method validated and accredited?#
