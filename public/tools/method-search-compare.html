    How the Method Search and Compare works

    Search matches the method name, technique, purpose and standards. Each result links to the full Method Library entry with the principle, validation status and related methods.

    Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it to a scientist.

    Questions

    Frequently asked.

    Can you run a method that is not listed?#
    Very likely. The library is growing towards 500+ methods; send us your method and we will confirm.
    Are these methods validated?#
    Compendial methods are verified for your product; non-compendial methods are validated to ICH Q2(R2). Accreditation scope is per site.
    Can I transfer my own method?#
    Yes, through a formal method transfer protocol.
